In brief

NS Scan has no ads, subscriptions or account. NS Labs adds no advertising, behavioral tracking or analytics SDK of its own and operates no document-collection backend. There is an important Google scanner exception: Google ML Kit and Google Play services may collect technical diagnostics and usage analytics. We do not promise zero third-party telemetry or fully offline first-use setup.

Documents and settings on your device

The app processes and stores scanned page images, generated PDFs, scan names and dates, folders, settings, backup status/receipts and your chosen folder permission locally to provide scanning, organization, editing and backup features. Gallery images are accessed when you choose to import them. Scans remain on your device unless you choose to share, export or back them up. NS Labs does not receive your documents, gallery, scan names, folder contents or local search history through an app server.

The app does not request its own camera or broad photo/storage permission: scanning uses Google Play services and file export/backup uses Android's system document picker. The app's own INTERNET permission is removed. That does not stop Google Play services or another document-provider app from using its own network permissions.

Google scanner and technical data

Document scanning uses Google's ML Kit Document Scanner through Google Play services. Google states that the document scanning flow operates on-device. Google Play services may download scanner models, logic and interface resources before first use.

Google's ML Kit disclosure describes collection of device information (manufacturer, model, operating system/build and available ML hardware), application package/version, device or installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration (such as image format/resolution), feature input/output sizes and versions, event types and error codes. Google uses this information for diagnostics and usage analytics. This is technical metadata, not a claim that Google uploads your scanned page contents.

Google states the documented ML Kit data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and is not transferred onward to third parties. NS Labs cannot disable or independently verify every Google Play services diagnostic behavior, does not provide an in-app opt-out for all such diagnostics, and does not control Google's retention or deletion practices. See:
- https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure
- https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/vision/doc-scanner
- https://policies.google.com/privacy

Sharing, export and optional backup

Sharing and Save to Files send your chosen PDF to an app or location you select. Backup is off until you choose a destination and request a backup or enable automatic backups. The app retains the Android folder access grant, not your cloud-provider password. Compatible local or cloud document providers receive PDF contents and file/folder names when you use them. Their own terms, privacy practices, transport security and retention apply; NS Labs does not operate that storage service. Future after-save backups occur only if you enable them. You may disable automatic backup or disconnect the destination in the app.

Backup is non-destructive PDF copying, not two-way sync or full-library restore. Older versions and earlier copies may remain in the destination. Successful writing to a provider does not guarantee that provider has finished uploading to its cloud.

Retention and deletion

Local scans remain until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall. You can delete individual scans or local folder contents in the app. Scan deletion and backup disconnection do not delete exported, shared or backup copies; delete those separately through the recipient/provider. Disconnecting backup releases the app's folder grant and cancels scheduled work. Export important PDFs before clearing data or uninstalling.

Version 1.3 disables implicit Android platform backup and specifies cloud/device-transfer exclusions. This change does not erase historical platform backups created by earlier versions. Device/OS behavior and any existing backup retention are controlled by their providers.

NS Labs has no server-side scan account or document archive to delete. Local scan deletion does not erase Google's technical diagnostics. Consult Google's privacy controls and the chosen cloud provider for their data requests; we do not promise that this app can delete their records or that Google data is deleted within a specific time.

Security

Android app-private storage and system-granted content access help restrict local file access. This app does not provide a separate encrypted vault, password protection or a guarantee of security on a compromised device. Google documents HTTPS for ML Kit telemetry. We cannot guarantee encryption practices for every external app or document provider you select.

Children

This general-purpose productivity app is not designed or marketed specifically for children. NS Labs does not knowingly solicit children's personal information. Its Google SDK and external-provider behavior is described above.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions contact Support email coming soon. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to answer your request; do not send sensitive documents unnecessarily. Any support correspondence is handled separately from automatic app collection. We may update this policy as functionality or third-party practices change and will update the effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the app and store disclosures where required.
