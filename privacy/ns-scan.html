NS Scan — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026
Publisher: NS Labs
Contact: github.com/nslabs-dev/nslabs-dev.github.io/issues
App: NS Scan, package app.nslabs.scan, version 1.5.1.

In brief

NS Scan has no ads, subscriptions or NS Labs account. NS Labs adds no advertising, behavioral tracking or analytics SDK of its own and operates no document-collection backend. Scans stay on your device unless you choose to share, export or back them up. There is an important Google scanner exception: Google ML Kit and Google Play services may collect technical diagnostics and usage analytics. We do not promise zero third-party telemetry or fully offline first-use setup.

Documents and settings on your device

The app processes and stores scanned page images, generated PDFs, scan names and dates, folders, settings, backup status and receipts, optional cloud connection records, and any folder permission you grant, so it can provide scanning, organization, editing and backup. Gallery images are accessed only when you choose to import them. NS Labs does not receive your documents, gallery, scan names, folder contents, search history or cloud credentials through an NS Labs server.

The app does not request its own camera or broad photo or media-storage permission. Scanning uses Google Play services. File export and folder backup use Android’s system document pickers. The app declares the INTERNET permission so optional direct cloud backup can use HTTPS when you connect a provider. That permission does not mean NS Labs operates a document backend, and it does not stop Google Play services or another document-provider app from using its own network permissions.

Google scanner and technical data

Document scanning uses Google’s ML Kit Document Scanner through Google Play services. Google states that the document scanning flow operates on-device. Google Play services may download scanner models, logic and interface resources before first use.

Google’s ML Kit disclosure describes collection of device information (manufacturer, model, operating system or build and available ML hardware), application package and version, device or installation identifiers, performance metrics, API configuration (such as image format or resolution), feature input and output sizes and versions, event types and error codes. Google uses this information for diagnostics and usage analytics. This is technical metadata, not a claim that Google uploads your scanned page contents.

Google states the documented ML Kit data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS and is not transferred onward to third parties. NS Labs cannot disable or independently verify every Google Play services diagnostic behavior, does not provide an in-app opt-out for all such diagnostics, and does not control Google’s retention or deletion practices. See:
- https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure
- https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/vision/doc-scanner
- https://policies.google.com/privacy

Sharing, export and optional backup

Sharing and Save to Files send your chosen PDF to an app or location you select. Backup is off until you choose a destination and request a backup or enable automatic backups.

Folder backup (Android Storage Access Framework). You may pick a local or cloud document-provider folder. The app retains the Android folder access grant, not a cloud-provider password. Compatible providers receive PDF contents and file or folder names when you use them. Their own terms, privacy practices, transport security and retention apply; NS Labs does not operate that storage service.

Optional direct cloud backup. When a provider is configured in the build you install, you may connect your own Google Drive, Dropbox or Microsoft OneDrive account from the app. Connection uses the provider’s sign-in flow in a system browser or native authorization UI. The app never sees your provider password. Direct backup sends PDFs and filenames over HTTPS to the account you authorize. Scopes are limited as follows when that provider is available:

  • Google Drive: drive.file (access to files the app creates or that you authorize; the app creates an NS Scan folder in your Drive).
  • Dropbox: files.content.write with scoped app-folder access (write into the app’s Dropbox folder, not your full Dropbox).
  • Microsoft OneDrive: Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder (the app’s OneDrive app folder).

The app may store encrypted OAuth tokens, account identifiers needed to renew access, connection status and local backup receipts on your device. Automatic after-save backups run only if you enable them. You may disable automatic backup or disconnect a destination in the app. Disconnect cancels queued work and clears the app’s local credentials for that provider; it does not delete copies already accepted by the provider. Online revocation may be unconfirmed when you are offline; you can also remove NS Scan access in the provider’s account settings.

Backup is non-destructive PDF copying, not two-way sync or full-library restore. Older versions and earlier copies may remain in the destination. Successful writing to a provider does not guarantee that provider has finished uploading to its cloud. Provider sign-in SDKs and services may process account, connection and diagnostic data under their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Local scans remain until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall. You can delete individual scans or local folder contents in the app. Scan deletion and backup disconnection do not delete exported, shared or backup copies; delete those separately through the recipient or provider. Disconnecting a folder backup releases the app’s folder grant and cancels scheduled work. Export important PDFs before clearing data or uninstalling.

The app disables implicit Android platform backup of its private data and specifies cloud and device-transfer exclusions. That does not erase historical platform backups created by earlier versions. Device and OS behavior and any existing backup retention are controlled by their providers.

NS Labs has no server-side scan account or document archive to delete. Local scan deletion does not erase Google’s technical diagnostics or provider-side copies. Consult Google’s privacy controls and the chosen cloud provider for their data requests; we do not promise that this app can delete their records or that Google or provider data is deleted within a specific time.

Security

Android app-private storage and system-granted content access help restrict local file access. OAuth tokens and related connection state are stored with Android Keystore-backed encryption where the app manages them; Microsoft’s sign-in library uses its own encrypted token cache. This app does not provide a separate encrypted vault, password protection for PDFs, or a guarantee of security on a compromised device. Direct cloud transfers use HTTPS. Google documents HTTPS for ML Kit telemetry. We cannot guarantee encryption practices for every external app or document provider you select.

Children

This general-purpose productivity app is not designed or marketed specifically for children. NS Labs does not knowingly solicit children’s personal information. Its Google SDK and external-provider behavior is described above.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, open an issue at github.com/nslabs-dev/nslabs-dev.github.io/issues. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to answer your request; do not send sensitive documents unnecessarily. Any support correspondence is handled separately from automatic app collection. We may update this policy as functionality or third-party practices change and will update the effective date. Material changes will be reflected in the app and store disclosures where required.
