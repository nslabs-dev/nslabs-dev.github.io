Features, requirements & limits

Scan your documents. Keep control of your files.

NS Scan by NS Labs turns paper documents into PDFs, with no ads, subscriptions, account or feature paywall. NS Labs does not collect your scans and adds no advertising or tracking analytics of its own.

Scan and save

  • Scan up to 20 pages per document using Google ML Kit's document scanner.
  • Use automatic edge detection, crop controls and scanner filters during capture.
  • Import pages from your gallery when gallery import is enabled.
  • Choose High, Medium or Small PDF quality for new scans and page-editor saves.

Organize your library

  • Create folders and move scans between them.
  • Search scan names, sort by name or date, and switch between list and grid views.
  • Rename scans and select multiple PDFs to move, share or delete.

Edit saved pages

  • Reorder pages with drag handles or arrow controls.
  • Remove pages with Undo, or append pages from the camera or gallery.
  • Save to rebuild your PDF, or cancel to leave the saved document unchanged.

Export on your terms

  • Share PDFs through Android's share sheet or use Save to Files.
  • Optionally choose a folder for manual or automatic PDF backups, including compatible cloud document providers.
  • Backup keeps earlier copies. It is not two-way sync or a full-library restore, and not every cloud app supports folder selection.

Make it yours

  • System, light and dark themes, with optional dynamic colors on Android 12 and later.
  • Custom file-name patterns using date and time tokens.

Privacy, with an honest exception

Your scans stay on your device unless you choose sharing, export or backup. The app has no account, ads or subscriptions, and no tracking in our own code.

Scanning runs on-device through Google Play services. The scanner module may need an internet download before first use. Google's ML Kit documentation describes technical diagnostics and usage analytics sent to Google, including device/app information, identifiers, performance and scanner events. We do not promise zero third-party telemetry or fully offline first-use setup. Your chosen cloud provider has its own privacy practices.

Requirements and limits

Android 6.0 or later, Google Play services and at least 1.7 GB total device RAM. Viewing PDFs externally needs a PDF reader. OCR, searchable text extraction and full-library restore are not included. Export or explicitly back up important PDFs before uninstalling; automatic Android platform backup is disabled.
